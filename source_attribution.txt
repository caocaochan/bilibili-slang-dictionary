7,205 slang and meme entries in this dictionary were informed by the
Chinese Meme Dictionary. Its Chinese glosses were translated and
the English dictionary definitions were independently edited. Changes were
made for concise learner-dictionary style and to reflect contextual usage.

English definitions from other harvested glossaries are retained where the
source supplied an English definition, as requested. Newly authored senses use
those sources as evidence. The project source-harvest ledger records each
source and its reuse notes; these sources do not all carry a redistribution
license.
